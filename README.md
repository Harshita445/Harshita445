## About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at **Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology**. I like building things people actually keep using, not just projects that look good for five minutes in a demo.

Most of what I work on sits somewhere between full stack development and applied AI, usually with a computer vision angle. I care a lot about the small details in a product: how a feature feels the second time someone uses it, not just whether it technically works the first time.

**Interests:** Artificial Intelligence · Machine Learning · Computer Vision · Full Stack Development · Product Development · Open Source · Human-Centered Technology

**Right now I'm focused on:** building products that solve real problems instead of collecting demo projects.

## Projects

### ✦ Alumly
An alumni mentorship platform that connects students with alumni for guidance, networking, and career support.

- Matches students with alumni based on shared interests and career paths
- Built around ongoing mentorship, not one-off introductions
- Focused on making career support and networking accessible to students who don't have industry connections yet

`React` `Next.js` `FastAPI` `SQLite`

<br/>

### ✦ Verve
A communication training platform for building stronger speaking skills through structured, repeated practice.

- Covers public speaking, storytelling, debate, and interview prep in one place
- Built around consistent practice rather than one-time tips
- Gives feedback that's meant to be acted on, not just read

<br/>

### ✦ Stellr
A timetable companion that compares schedules between friends and finds shared free periods automatically.

- Solves a genuinely annoying problem: manually comparing timetables to find a common free slot
- Instantly surfaces overlapping free time between multiple people's schedules
- Makes planning study sessions, group work, and meetups a lot less painful
