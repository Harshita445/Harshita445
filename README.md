## About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at **Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology**, focused on building software that solves real, practical problems — not just polished demos.

My work sits at the intersection of **full stack development** and **applied AI/ML**, with a particular interest in computer vision and human-centered product design. I care about the details that make a product actually usable: clean architecture, thoughtful UX, and features people return to.

**Currently exploring:** Artificial Intelligence · Computer Vision · Full Stack Development · Open Source · Human-Centered Technology
